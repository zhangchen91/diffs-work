Reviewing a PPT has two common pitfalls: **the slide order changed**, and **half a sentence changed on
one slide**. Comparing page by page turns "one slide was moved" into "every slide after it changed",
so slides are aligned first, and only then are the texts compared.

## What it compares

It reads the text boxes and their text (`a:t`) in `ppt/slides/slideN.xml`, plus the **slide order** in
`ppt/presentation.xml`:

- each slide's **title** and body bullets (notes outside the body text and page numbers do not
  count);
- the order of slides;
- speaker notes (an optional switch; off by default).

Not read: layouts, masters, animations, transitions, images.

## How it decides

<Doc kind="step" n="1" title="Align by slide" />

Using each slide's **title** as the primary key and the slide's full text as a tie-breaker, it finds
where "the same slide" sits on each side. No counterpart found → the slide was added or deleted.

<Doc kind="step" n="2" title="Compare within a slide" />

Text on the same slide is compared **paragraph** by paragraph: bullets added, removed, rewritten. A
rewrite likewise requires "a long enough common prefix and suffix to count as an edit", and the
changed characters are marked within the paragraph.

<Doc kind="step" n="3" title="Whole-slide relocation" />

A slide disappears from its old place and reappears elsewhere unchanged, with identical content → a
**move**, stating "slide N → slide M". This is very common when reordering "conclusion first or data
first", and it is the change people most easily overlook.

## What the result looks like

```jsonc
{
  "kind": "pptx",
  "slides": [
    { "index": 7, "title": "Financial forecast", "status": "changed",
      "changes": [ { "op": "mod", "old": "2026 revenue 120M", "new": "2026 revenue 150M" } ] },
    { "index": 3, "title": "The team", "status": "moved", "from": 9 }
  ]
}
```

## What it cannot see

| Cannot see | Why |
|---|---|
| Pixel differences in images and charts | That is image comparison |
| Subtle movement of layouts and positions | Layout adjustments are not content changes |
| Animations / transitions | Unrelated to content |
| Notes pages | Off by default, can be switched on |

<Callout tone="note" title="On 'same number of slides, different order'.">
This is the most common and most easily missed kind of change. The view orders rows by the alignment
result rather than by page number, so you can see at a glance that "slide 9 became slide 3" instead
of "slides 3 through 9 all changed".
</Callout>

## Common questions

**Every slide shows "delete + add"?** Most likely the titles sit in different text boxes, or the two
export tools split the title into different runs. Check first whether the aligned titles in the view
match.

**Only one slide changed, but the whole deck is red?** Check whether a deleted slide threw the
alignment off; the view lists "slide N deleted" explicitly in the change list.
